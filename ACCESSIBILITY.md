# Accessibility\n\nRK Riad Khan aims to keep its website and documentation usable by as many people as possible. We use semantic HTML, keyboard-friendly controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, alternative text for meaningful images, and reduced-motion support where appropriate.\n\nIf you find an accessibility barrier, please open an issue in the [source repository](https://github.com/rkriad585) with the page, device, browser, and steps to reproduce.\n
